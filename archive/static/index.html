01 / Tokenization explained

↗

Real-world assets.
Represented digitally.

Tokenization represents ownership or rights over an asset as digital tokens on a blockchain. What each token represents depends on the asset and how the issuer structures it.

OpenAssets provides the technology to issue and manage those tokens, with programmable controls for asset transfers and participant eligibility.

03 / From idea to issuance

↗

Plan your issuance.
Step by step.

Use these four stages to frame your project discussion. The scope will depend on your asset and requirements.

01Define your asset+

Identify the underlying asset and the rights each token will represent. Define who can participate and what they need to do.

02Design the framework+

Map your issuance structure, participant checks, and transfer rules. Establish the legal and operational requirements the technology must support.

03Configure & issue+

Set up the token and its controls. Test the issuance workflow before making tokens available to eligible participants.

04Manage & connect+

Manage participants and asset operations after issuance. Review the integrations and controls needed as your project develops.
